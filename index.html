Terms & Conditions

Important Terms You Should Know.

One Active Request at a Time

For all requests: avg. turnaround time 24–72 hrs*
  • To ensure quality and faster delivery, we work on one active request at a time.
  • Once the current request is approved or completed, the next request in your queue begins.
  • All requests are completed based on the order in which they are submitted, unless otherwise agreed.

Every Request Has Different Complexity

  • Some requests may take a few hours, while others require several days depending on their complexity, content, and revisions.

Revision Policy

  • Unlimited revisions apply only to the approved concept.
  • A complete redesign or change in the original brief is considered a new request.
  • Adaptations, corrections or updates to an old / already completed design file or video — 2 requests are considered as 1 request.

Refund Policy

  • Once project work or printing has been initiated or is in progress, any advance payment or full payment is non-refundable under any circumstances.
  • A full refund or subscription request return will be issued only if Graphic Crow fails to deliver the project within 2 days after the agreed deadline, provided the delay is solely our responsibility and not due to changes, delays, or pending approvals from the client.

Print Delivery Policy

  • Delivery timelines are estimated and may vary depending on the courier service, location, weather conditions, holidays, or other unforeseen circumstances.
  • Once the printed materials are handed over to the courier or delivery partner, Graphic Crow is not responsible for delays caused by the shipping provider.
  • Customers are requested to inspect the package upon delivery and report any transit damage or missing items within 24 hours of receiving the order, along with photos for verification.
  • Shipping charges, if applicable, are non-refundable once the order has been dispatched.
